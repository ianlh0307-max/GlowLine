Our Mission

Redefining care, one room at a time

We started GlowLine after learning that 1 in 13 nurses find it difficult to identify medical wires in a dark patient room. Our team set out to design a solution that keeps rooms dark for patient recovery while giving clinical staff instant, reliable line identification — without new equipment or added training.

We've since spoken with 25+ nurses and clinicians to validate the problem and refine the product, and mapped a Class I regulatory pathway to bring GlowLine to hospitals safely and efficiently.
